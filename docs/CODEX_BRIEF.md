# PANEL ZERO — Master Art Brief (Codex batches)

Every batch below is a standalone prompt to paste into Codex. Upload each
resulting zip to the chat; integration is automatic (WebP conversion,
anchoring metadata, cache updates). File NAMES must match exactly — they are
the game's lookup keys.

## Global style header (paste at the top of EVERY batch)

> High-resolution game sprites for a manga action game, Japanese anime style:
> bold clean lineart, rich colors, crisp cel shading, dramatic rim light.
> Every image on a FULLY TRANSPARENT background, PNG, 1024×1024. No text, no
> watermarks, no borders, no drop shadows baked in. Characters face RIGHT
> unless stated. Feet/base of the object at the bottom of the frame.
>
> ANIMATION FRAMES: files named `<action>-f1.png`, `-f2.png`, `-f3.png` are
> stop-motion frames of ONE action. The character must be IDENTICAL across
> frames — same outfit, colors, proportions, scale, and ground line — with
> ONLY the limbs/hair/effects moving. Think anime shot on threes.

## Character reference (keep consistent with the sprites you already made)

- **KAI** — 14-year-old shonen protagonist. Spiky dark navy-blue hair, orange
  eyes, red zip jacket over cream tee, dark jeans, red-and-white sneakers.
- **SUMI** — ghost-girl of living ink. Enormous flowing indigo hair that
  dissolves into ink swirls and blue flame licks, pale glowing face, no feet —
  she trails into ink.
- **THE REJECTED ONE (REJ)** — scrapped anti-hero. Long white hair, red eyes,
  black high-collar coat, gaunt, cracked-porcelain vibe.

  scar, hand wraps, torn brown gi pants, barefoot.
- **YURI** — reality-bender draft. Waist-length white hair, cold violet eyes,
  slim dark-purple long coat, elegant and still.
- **GOMA** — gag-manga blob. Cream-colored round mochi body, stubby arms/feet,
  big band-aid on head, huge expressive eyes.
- **THE ARTIST (Aoki-sensei)** — exhausted young mangaka. Messy dark bun,
  blue cardigan, gray tee, black joggers, barefoot.
- **THE LEVIATHAN** — sea serpent of living ink (already made).
- **THE SMUDGE** — small scribble-monster: a chaotic ball of pencil scribbles
  with angry white eyes.
- **THE HAND** — the Artist's giant hand holding a white eraser, photoreal-ish
  scale monster of the story (already exists as hand-eraser).

## Integration notes (for the repo, not for Codex)

- Frames auto-animate: `drawSprite('kai-run', ...)` cycles `kai-run-f1..fN`
  at ~7 fps via the shared sprite clock when frames exist in meta.json.
- Hazard sprites are looked up as `fx-<obj>`; props as `prop-<type>`.
- New pose names (kai-hit, kai-jump, ken-punch, …) need mapping entries in
  js/chars.js pose→sprite tables when first used by chapter data.
- Textures land as pattern fills in js/art.js `tonePattern` (swap the
  procedural tile for the painted one when present).
